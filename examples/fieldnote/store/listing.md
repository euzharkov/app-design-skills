### Description (4000)
```
Fieldnote is a private journal that asks for less: one honest sentence about today. No blank pages, no streaks, no scores.

DONE IN UNDER A MINUTE
Write a sentence, add a tag if you like, save. That's the whole habit.

YOUR WEEK, READ BACK GENTLY
Every Sunday, Fieldnote shows what came up most in plain words, and one line of yours worth rereading.

CALM AT THE END OF THE DAY
Made for evenings: quiet type, a warm dark theme, nothing that pings or nags.

NO STREAKS TO KEEP
Missed days are just days. A quiet week is still a week, and there's nothing to catch up on.

YOURS, AND ONLY YOURS
No account and no analytics. Lock Fieldnote with Face ID. Notes leave your phone only if you turn on end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync or export them.

FIELDNOTE PLUS
Writing, tags and the weekly look-back are free. Plus adds the Mac app and look-backs across months and years.
```
